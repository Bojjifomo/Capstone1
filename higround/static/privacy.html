Privacy policy

Data lu, aturannya jelas.

Last updated 23 November 2026 · English below

Ringkasnya

  • Yang kami ambil: nama lu di intervals.icu dan data aktivitas lari: tanggal & jam, tipe, nama aktivitas, jarak, durasi, elevasi, detak jantung rata-rata/maks, dan nama perangkat. Cuma lewat izin ACTIVITY:READ yang lu kasih.
  • Yang nggak kami ambil: rute/GPS, data kesehatan lain, email, password, atau data apa pun di luar aktivitas.
  • Buat apa: leaderboard klub dan dashboard performa lu. Itu aja.
  • Siapa yang lihat: leaderboard (nama, total km, jumlah run, level) bisa dilihat siapa aja yang punya link web ini. Profil dan daftar run lu cuma bisa dilihat member yang sudah login. Lu bisa sembunyikan diri dari leaderboard dan profil kapan aja di Pengaturan.
  • Nggak dijual, nggak dibagi ke pihak ketiga, nggak dipakai buat iklan, dan nggak dipakai buat melatih atau menjalankan AI.
  • Disimpan di mana: di server milik pengurus klub (database lokal). Akses cuma lewat HTTPS.
  • Hapus kapan aja: Dashboard → Pengaturan → Putuskan & hapus data gue. Akun dan semua run lu dihapus permanen, dan akses ke intervals.icu dicabut. Lu juga bisa cabut akses dari intervals.icu → Settings → Apps.
  • Kontak: pengurus HiGround Run Club, lewat DM Instagram @higroundcoffee.

In English

What we collect. Your intervals.icu display name and athlete id, and your running activities via the ACTIVITY:READ scope you grant: start date and time, type, name, distance, duration, elevation gain, average/max heart rate and device name. We do not read GPS tracks, wellness data, your email or your password.

Why. To show a monthly mileage leaderboard for the HiGround Run Club community and a personal training dashboard. Nothing else.

Who can see it. The leaderboard (name, distance totals, run count, level) is visible to anyone with the link to this site. Your profile and run list are visible only to signed-in club members. You can hide yourself from the leaderboard and profiles at any time in Settings.

Sharing. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train or run AI models.

Storage and security. Data is stored in a local database on a server run by the club organisers and is only served over HTTPS. Access tokens are stored server-side and never shown in the browser.

Deletion. Use Dashboard → Settings → “Disconnect & delete my data” to permanently delete your account and all your runs and revoke our intervals.icu access. You can also revoke access from intervals.icu → Settings → Apps; we then stop syncing.

Garmin. Activities recorded on Garmin devices are shown with Garmin attribution. Garmin and Garmin device names are trademarks of Garmin Ltd.

Contact. The HiGround Run Club organisers, via Instagram DM to @higroundcoffee.
